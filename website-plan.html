04
Speed and wording

Fast on a phone, safe under the advertising rules.

Google ranks fast pages higher, and most of your clients are on their phones. Google's own standard for a good page is the main image showing within 2.5 seconds.

Home page, main image on a phone
8 to 13 seconds

Three standard mobile speed tests on 11 September. Most of the home page weight is platform scripts. Across the site, 9 images are over 500 KB, the largest almost 3.7 MB.

The rebuild
Target: 2.5 seconds

Images sized for phones, only the scripts a page needs, and a speed check on every page before it goes live.

Advertising rules
"Specialist" is a link in the footer of every page, and appears 20 times across the cosmetic tattoo, brows and skin pages.
Every page written with AHPRA-safe wording and checked against the cosmetic advertising rules before it goes live.
