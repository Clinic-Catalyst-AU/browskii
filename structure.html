How Browskii and MOMO share one website and still track as two.

This is the picture I was drawing in my head on our call. Browskii and MOMO stay together as one website on the one domain, so nothing you have built in Google is lost and a brow client can still wander across to skin. What stays together

The things you were afraid to lose.

  • The domain and its history. Every "browskii" search keeps landing on you. Nothing moves, so nothing drops.
  • One Google Business Profile. Your 4.9 and 168 reviews stay on one listing, with MOMO's services added to it.
  • One booking system, one client list. Every client sits in the one CRM with a tag for which door they came through, so a brow client can be offered skin and you can see it happen.
  • Brow clients finding skin. The home page shows both doors. Every Browskii page has a way into MOMO and every MOMO page has a way back, so the brow client who would have discovered skin still does.
  • One chat on both sites. The same chat window sits on every Browskii and MOMO page, answers questions, books appointments and passes people across to the other brand. See how it looks
  • One brand family. Browskii's warm neutrals and MOMO's burgundy and blue sit side by side on the same site.
What stays separate

The data footprint, so ads and SEO work.

  • Where the ads land. A MOMO ad only ever lands on a MOMO page inside /momo/, never the home page. A brow ad lands on a brow page. Each offer gets its own page.
  • How Meta learns. Same pixel, separate conversion events: a Browskii lead and a MOMO lead are counted apart, and each brand runs its own campaigns and ad sets. Meta learns two audiences instead of blurring them into one.
  • How Google reports. The /momo/ pages sit in their own group in Google Analytics and Search Console, so you see MOMO's traffic, rankings and enquiries on their own line, next to Browskii's.
  • What each side chases in search. Browskii pages go after cosmetic tattoo, nano brows and lip blush in Brisbane. MOMO pages go after rosacea treatment, skin clinic, IPL and skin needling in Newstead. No page tries to do both.
  • The social accounts. @browskii.au and @momo_by_browskii.au stay as they are, each feeding its own pages.
  • The money. MOMO's leads, bookings and revenue are reported as their own line from month one.
One chat on both sites

The same chat window on Browskii and MOMO.

Whichever site someone lands on, the chat in the corner is the same one. It answers questions at any hour, books the appointment, and knows both menus. A brow client who asks about redness gets offered the skin assessment. A MOMO client who asks about brows gets taken to their Browskii booking. Right now your DMs and emails go to whoever gets to them first. This way nobody who messages at 9pm waits until morning, and your team can step into any conversation and take it from there, as you do best.

  • Moves people across both brands. Every chat can hand a client to the other side when that is what they are asking for.
  • One client record. The conversation saves to the one client list, tagged with the door it came through, so MOMO's numbers stay MOMO's.
  • Trained on your own words. Your menus, prices, aftercare, policies and FAQs, checked against the advertising rules before it goes live.

And if one day you do want MOMO fully on its own

Because MOMO already has its own pages, its own tracking, its own list and its own numbers, moving it to its own domain later is a redirect job, not a rebuild. Every /momo/ page points to its new home, Google follows the redirects, and the history comes with it. Nothing has to be decided now.
