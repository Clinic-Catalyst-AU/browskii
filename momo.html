Questions we get every week

Before you book.

Is this for rosacea?+

Yes. Redness, flushing, reactive skin and rosacea, diagnosed or suspected, are the skin MOMO sees most and the skin Ashleigh has a special interest in. If you have a diagnosis from your doctor, bring it. If you do not, the consultation is where we work out what is going on.

I was prescribed something for it. Can I still come?+

Yes. Many of the people Ashleigh sees were offered a prescription and would rather not stay on it. Bring what you were given and what you decided. Nothing at MOMO replaces your doctor's advice, and the consultation is where the two get talked through properly.

Will treatment hurt or make the redness worse?+

Reactive skin gets treated gently or not at all. Nothing goes on your skin until we have seen it and talked it through, and the first phase of any plan is calming. You will know exactly what to expect before anything happens.

Can I keep wearing makeup?+

Usually, yes, and part of the plan is often changing what you wear so it stops irritating the skin underneath. We talk about it at the consultation.

How many visits will I need?+

It depends on your skin, which is why we do not quote a number on a website. Your written plan sets it out after the consultation, and we review it as your skin changes.

Do I need to stop my current skincare first?+

Do not change anything before your consultation. Bring what you use, or photos of it, and we go through it together. Often the first win is removing one product.

Is there a booking fee?+

Booking fees and the cancellation policy are explained before you confirm, so there are no surprises. Cancellations need to be phoned through on 0417 322 366.
