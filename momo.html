Questions we get every week

Before you book.

Is this for rosacea?+

Yes. Redness, flushing, reactivity and rosacea, diagnosed or suspected, are the skin MOMO sees most, and the skin Ashleigh has a special interest in. If you have a diagnosis from your doctor, bring it. If you do not, the consultation is where we work out what is going on.

Will treatment hurt or make the redness worse?+

Reactive skin gets treated gently or not at all. Nothing goes on your skin until we have seen it and talked it through, and the first phase of any plan is calming, not pushing. You will know exactly what to expect before anything happens.

Can I keep wearing makeup?+

Usually, yes, and part of the plan is often changing what you wear so it stops irritating the skin underneath. We talk about it at the consultation.

How many visits will I need?+

It depends on your skin, which is why we do not quote a number on a website. Your written plan sets it out after the consultation, and we review it as your skin changes.

Do I need to stop my current skincare?+

Do not change anything before your consultation. Bring what you use, or photos of it, and we will go through it together. Often the first win is removing something, not adding something.

Is there a booking fee?+

Booking fees and the cancellation policy are explained before you confirm, so there are no surprises. Cancellations need to be phoned through on 0417 322 366.
